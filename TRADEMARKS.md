## Protected marks

- the name **Sample Grabber**
- the name **Mythen Studios**
- the Sample Grabber logo and mark (waveform with red trim brackets), the app icon and the
  brand assets in `brand/` and `installer/images/`

## What you may do

- Talk and write about Sample Grabber, review it, link to it, make tutorials and videos.
- Say that your project is "based on Sample Grabber" or "a fork of Sample Grabber".
- Distribute unmodified official releases under their original name, together with the source code.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Publish a modified version, a fork or a derived product under the name "Sample Grabber",
  "Mythen Studios" or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the logo, icon or brand assets for a modified version or for another product.
- Suggest that your version is official, endorsed by or affiliated with Mythen Studios.

If you fork the project, choose your own name and replace the logo, the icon and the brand assets
(`brand/`, `installer/images/`, the icons referenced in `CMakeLists.txt`) before you distribute it.
